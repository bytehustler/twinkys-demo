Twinky's demo - independent concept, not an official website.
Run: open index.html, or `python3 -m http.server 8000` and visit http://localhost:8000
Photos: drop your own files into assets/ named hero.jpg, about.jpg, gallery-1.jpg ... gallery-6.jpg.
They are picked up automatically (the .svg files are only fallbacks). See the comment at the top of index.html.
Themes: ?theme=rose or ?theme=emerald. Remove the switcher before going live.
