About Twinky's

A destination for hair, beauty & skin

Twinky's is a unisex salon and beauty studio located at City Point Complex in Kolhapur, bringing hair, beauty and skin care together under one roof.

Whether you are visiting for a fresh look, a little self-care or preparation for a special occasion, the aim is simple: a welcoming space and attentive service from the moment you walk in.
